Weak-Motor Swing-Up Lab

A pendulum and a cart-pole, each driven by a DC motor too weak to lift the pole directly. Every controller has to pump energy over several swings and then catch the pole upright. The physics below is the same model the controllers were trained on, integrated live in your browser.

pendulum stall torque 0.25 N·m = 42% of mgl back-EMF: torque → 0 at 20 rad/s cart force 4 N, rail ±0.40 m control 50 Hz, |u| ≤ 1

Success = upright (|θ| < 0.15 rad, |ω| < 1 rad/s, cart inside the rail) for the last 1.5 s, over 20 fixed starts. Cost = Σ ℓ(x,u)·dt with ℓ = (1−cos θ) + 0.01ω² + 0.05u² (+ cart terms), the same objective for every method. DP is the numerically exact optimum for the pendulum.
